Privacy notice

Last updated: 23 October 2026

What we store

  • Account: your email address, an optional display name, and a password (stored only as a salted hash by our authentication provider).
  • Your prep data: problem status, stars, notes, confidence ratings, solve dates, and your notepads (text, sketches and saved links).
  • In your browser only: your theme and last-opened tab, plus the login session token.

We don't use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and we don't sell or share your data.

Why

Only to provide the service: to sign you in and to save your progress so it's available on any device.

Where

Data is stored with Supabase (database and authentication) and the site is served by Vercel. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest. Access is restricted so that each account can read and change only its own data.

Your choices

  • Access and portability: open your account (click your name in the sidebar) and choose Export my data.
  • Deletion: choose Delete my account in the same place. Your account and all associated data are deleted immediately.
  • Correction: you can edit or remove any of your notes and progress at any time.

Retention

Data is kept while your account exists and deleted when you delete your account. Provider backups roll off according to their retention schedule.

Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/keshav2608-rgb/amzprep.
